Mercaz

Jewish life, centered

Mercaz is an app for keeping each day's Jewish practice by Chabad custom: the day's times for where you are, your practices and their reminders, a siddur, a morning alarm, and quiet over Shabbat and Yom Tov.

Support

For questions, problems or suggestions, write to admin@open-civ.com.

If you are testing Mercaz through TestFlight, you can also send feedback from the TestFlight app, or take a screenshot in Mercaz and choose Share Beta Feedback.

Privacy

Mercaz has no account, no analytics and no server of its own, and its makers collect no information about you. Read the privacy policy.
