Mercaz

Privacy Policy

Effective: 15 November 2026

Mercaz is an app for keeping daily Jewish practice by Chabad custom. It has no account, no analytics and no server of its own. We, the makers of Mercaz, do not collect any information about you.

What Mercaz keeps, and where

Everything Mercaz knows about you is kept on your own devices and in your own iCloud account.

  • Your record: whether you set it up as a man or a woman, the level you chose, your practices and their reminders, the practices you marked done and when, the city you chose if you chose one, the personal dates you added (such as a yahrzeit), and your settings for the siddur and for Shabbat and Yom Tov mode.
  • On this iPhone only: the place found from your phone's location, when you let Mercaz follow it; the day's times worked out from it; and what the widgets and the Screen Time extensions need to show the day. (A copy of the day is also kept on your own Apple Watch, if you have one; see below.)

If iCloud is on for Mercaz, your record is kept in your private iCloud database, so that it is there on your other devices and after you replace your phone. Apple keeps that database for you; we cannot see it. If iCloud is off, your record stays on this iPhone.

What leaves your iPhone

Nothing leaves your iPhone for us. The only things that leave it go to Apple, through Apple's own services, or to your own Apple Watch, and only when the app needs them:

  • iCloud, to keep your record in your own private database, as above.
  • Apple's maps services, to find the name of the place your phone is in, and to search for a city when you type one. To name the place, your phone's location is sent to Apple's maps service; it is never sent to us.
  • Your Apple Watch, if you have one paired with this iPhone: the iPhone sends it the day (its times and your practices) so that the Watch can show it, and the Watch sends back what you mark on it.
  • The App Store, when you subscribe, restore a purchase or the app checks your subscription. Apple handles the payment; Mercaz never sees your payment details.

Apple's handling of these is described in Apple's own privacy policy.

What never leaves your iPhone

  • Your calendar. If you let Mercaz read your calendar, it reads your events on this iPhone to show when one leaves no time for a prayer or runs into Shabbat or a festival. It never writes to your calendar, never stores your events, and never sends them anywhere.
  • The day's times. They are worked out on this iPhone from your place; no server works them out for you.
  • The app's own log, which records what the app did, for finding faults. It stays on the device.

What Mercaz does not do

  • It does not track you, across apps or websites or at all.
  • It has no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporter of its own and no third-party code that collects data.
  • It does not sell, share or rent any information, because it has none to give.

Permissions

Mercaz asks for permission to use your location, to send notifications, to set an alarm, to read your calendar and to use Screen Time, each only when you turn on the feature that needs it. You can withdraw any of them at any time in the iPhone's Settings; the app keeps working without it.

Removing your information

Deleting Mercaz removes what it keeps on your iPhone. To remove your record from iCloud as well, open the iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then the storage used by your apps, choose Mercaz and delete its data. A subscription is managed and cancelled in the App Store, under your account's subscriptions.

Children

Mercaz collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If Mercaz ever changes how it handles information, this page will be updated before the version that changes it is released, and the date at the top will change.
